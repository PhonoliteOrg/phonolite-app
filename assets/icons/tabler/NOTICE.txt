Tabler Icons
Source: https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons
License: MIT; see LICENSE.

These SVGs contain the app's Tabler icons and Phonolite variants.
Flutter loads the files through lib/widgets/ui/svg_asset_icon.dart. Edit the SVG
to change the icon; no Dart path generation or asset generation step is needed.

playlist.svg                Playlists navigation
playlist-add.svg            Add to playlist
playlist-edit.svg           Track editing in playlists and Liked songs
brand-google-analytics.svg  Stats navigation and settings
stats-reset-device.svg      Device-only listening history reset
stats-reset-account.svg     Account-wide listening history reset
cloud.svg                   Server settings and source selection
reload.svg                  Library and server refresh
edit.svg                    Playlist metadata and management; collection/settings editing

playlist-edit.svg is a Phonolite variant matching the playlist-add linework,
with a pencil in place of the add symbol.
playlist-add.svg uses a centered viewport to match the visible size of the
neighboring Lucide playback controls.
playlist.svg uses the same list with a compact filled eighth note, sized to
match the plus and pencil symbols.
The playlist, playlist-add, and playlist-edit artwork fills the same area as
the other icons. All three use identical bars at y=3, 9, and 15 with a 2-unit
stroke and round caps. The note, plus, and pencil are approximately 10 units
tall and occupy the same lower-right area.

The Stats variants keep the tallest bar in the middle. The reset variants use
a small monitor or account symbol over the chart's lower-right corner, with
a transparent gap around the symbol to keep the linework separate.
