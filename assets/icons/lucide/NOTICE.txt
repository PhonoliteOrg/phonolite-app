Lucide 1.52.0
Official source: https://lucide.dev/guide/static
The app bundles these SVGs and a subset of the official icon font for offline use.
The font subset centers each glyph within its square advance cell.
heart-filled.svg is a Phonolite variant of heart.svg with a filled interior.
LICENSE contains the Lucide ISC and inherited Feather MIT notices.
manifest.json records the full replacement mapping and package integrity.
Regenerate: python tools/vendor_lucide.py --download
Then: node tools/render_lucide_native.cjs <path-to-sharp-module>
Finally: dart format lib/widgets/ui/phonolite_icons.dart
Original Phonolite branding and launcher assets are retained.
